---BIO---

Hello! I’m a PhD student at LSU studying mathematics.
I’m broadly interested in topology and combinatorics, with a particular fondness for arrangements.
Additionally, I’m into graph theory and, every now and then, I’ve dabbled in knot theory.

I like getting involved in my community.
I currently serve as secretary and webmaster of <a href="https://www.math.lsu.edu/~awm/" target="_blank">LSU’s AWM chapter</a>, and as webmaster for <a href="https://www.math.lsu.edu/~trm-rtg/" target="_blank">LSU’s RTG</a>.
I also organise the Informal Combinatorics Seminar and regularly participate in the Informal Geometry and Topology seminar.
