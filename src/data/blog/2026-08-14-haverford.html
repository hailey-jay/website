Hey yall!

I recently returned from yet another summer adventure! This time, I was up at Haverford College, TA'ing for an REU. It was sponsored by Budapest Semesters in Mathematics and the professor for our group was the brilliant (and extremely friendly) Josh Sabloff. Everyone in the group was beyond wonderful; shout-out to Peter Ahlin, Catherine DiLeo, Olivia McGrew, and Rishubh Thaper for their sharp minds, work ethic, and collaborative spirit! I loved working with yall and would love to see yall again.

[image strawbridge | Strawbridge Observatory through the brush]

More than just a fun summer project, we ended up doing really good research! Keep your eyes peeled for that, but it's still a work-in-progress. I was pleasantly surprised that everyone in the group was more than strong enough to hold their own in serious math research, even through big challenges and many, many failed Reidemeister moves. I feel confident in being able to call them my collaborators.

On a personal note, I got to rediscover my love of soccer! We all spent time watching the World Cup and took evenings off to kick the ball around. I'm told "math soccer" isn't normally as competitive and high-quality! Hopefully, I'll spend some time this semester continuing to play.

This post would not be complete without mentioning my good friends in the "other" group: the professors Aaron Abrams and Jamie Pommersheim, the TA Annie Holden, and participants Aren Gürlap, Sofia Piccirillo, and Nickey Zhang. Yall more than impressed me and everyone else there.

I've done my very best to provide links to everyone to give them all the proper credit. If I haven't, I'm sure they'll let me know.

Two special mentions from the above: Catherine is an organizer of the National Set Championship at the JMM every year. The preceding link is stale, so go sign up for the upcoming one whenever that's available. Additionally, Aren is an officer and writer for the Brown Puzzlehunt.
