Hey yall!

I promise this isn't meant to become a travel blog, but... this is once again about a trip! This time, I went to Eugene for a conference and then stayed with my aunt and uncle in Portland.

I was pleasantly surprised at the temperature. Even in August, it was chilly enough to want a hoodie at times. With Baton Rouge consistently above 110 in the heat index, it was deeply refreshing to be able to walk outside comfortably. The nature was similarly stunning, and walking along the Willamette River was lovely. All in all, I think Eugene might be one of my favourite places in the world.

[image willamette | The Willamette River]

As with all of my trips, I'm really happy about all the folks I got to meet. I made new friends and connections, and had a really wonderful dinner with all the conference attendees! Scott, I'll submit a pull request soon, I promise. The University of Oregon was itself beautiful, and, while under extensive construction and revitalisation, is among the prettiest campuses I've seen.

[image uoregon | A wooded campus]

Portland was similarly pleasant. I have rather severe food allergies, but every place was either accommodating or simply vegan and fortunately devoid of peanuts. I particularly enjoyed getting my morning coffee and muffin at Jet Black. If you find yourself in the area, show them some love! Additionally, I somehow managed to survive a burger at McMenamins; moreover, it was a pretty good burger!

Parts of the city were very strange to someone from the South. The city is organised around neighbourhoods, each with their own "main street" area. Walking and biking seemed to be sufficient for daily life rather than just as exercise.

My favourite place in the city, however, was the International Rose Test Garden. Roses are my favourite flower (check the site favicon!) and one finds oneself surrounded by a menagerie of rose variants, each more beautiful than the last.
